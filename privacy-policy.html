Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Affordable Property Servicez LLC ("APS," "we," "us," or "our") operates this website (the "Site") to share information about our landscaping and property services in Orlando, FL and to let visitors request quotes and get in touch with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the Site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you fill out our Contact form or Free Quote form, we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, email address, phone number, service you're interested in, and any details you type into the message field. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect any information beyond what you submit through these forms.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your inquiry, provide a quote, and communicate with you about your project. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services We Use

To operate the Site, we rely on a small number of third-party services:

  • Web3Forms — our Contact and Free Quote forms are processed through Web3Forms (web3forms.com), a third-party form delivery service that securely sends your submission to our email inbox. Web3Forms may process your submitted data according to its own privacy policy.
  • Google Maps — several pages embed a Google Maps view to show our service area. Loading this embedded map may allow Google to set its own cookies or collect data according to Google's privacy policy, independent of this Site.
  • Google Fonts & Font Awesome — we load fonts and icons from Google Fonts and the Cloudflare CDN (cdnjs) to display the Site correctly. These are standard web font/icon deliveries and do not collect personal information beyond typical web request data (such as IP address) inherent to loading any web resource.

Cookies

This Site does not itself set any cookies, use analytics tracking, or use advertising pixels. We do not use your browser's local storage to track you. The only cookies that may be set while visiting this Site come from the embedded Google Maps view described above, which is controlled by Google, not by us.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through our Site, including serving the Site exclusively over HTTPS. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

If you've submitted a form and would like us to delete the information you provided, contact us using the details below and we will remove it from our records.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our practices. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
